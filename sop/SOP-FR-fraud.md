# SOP-FR: Scam and fraud disputes (mock)

## SOP-FR-01 Scam victim
Treat the customer as a possible victim. Request a temporary hold on any available balance (capped).
Route the case to the fraud team. Never ask for or accept the customer's PIN or OTP.

## SOP-FR-02 AML referral
Send the receiving wallet (or the claimant, for a double-recovery claim) to the AML/CFT team. Only the
AML/CFT team decides whether to file a suspicious transaction report with BFIU.

## SOP-FR-03 No tipping off
No message to a wallet under review may mention suspicion, fraud, investigation or police
(Money Laundering Prevention Act 2012 §6).

## SOP-FR-04 Double-recovery claims
If the ledger shows the amount was already sent back after the original transfer, reject the claim with
a factual explanation. A supervisor must approve the rejection.
