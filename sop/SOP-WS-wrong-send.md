# SOP-WS: Wrong-send disputes (mock)

## SOP-WS-01 Intake
Record the complaint on any channel (app, call, SMS, email). Confirm the customer has seen the notice
(purpose, retention, who sees it, how to withdraw). Give the case number and the 10-working-day deadline.

## SOP-WS-02 Temporary hold
If the money is still in the receiving wallet, an authorised officer may place a temporary hold capped
at the disputed amount and at the available balance. Ferot only raises the request; the officer acts in
the core system.

## SOP-WS-03 Recipient consent
Contact the recipient with a neutral message asking for consent to return the money. Do not accuse the
recipient. Record the answer.

## SOP-WS-04 Legal route
If the recipient refuses or cannot be reached, inform the customer that a return then depends on legal
process and that they may file a General Diary (GD) at the nearest police station.

## SOP-WS-05 Closure
Close within 10 working days (Bangladesh MFS Regulations 2022 §17.3). Tell the customer the outcome and
how to escalate to Bangladesh Bank if they are not satisfied.
