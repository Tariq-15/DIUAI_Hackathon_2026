# Ferot — AI wrong-send & dispute copilot (built for upay)

> AI Hackathon 2026 (AI DEV FEST, DIU CPC × upay) · Track 06: Operations & Service Intelligence
> **Prototype. Synthetic data only. Not an official upay product or message.**

Ferot (ফেরত, "money back") helps an MFS support team resolve "I sent money to the wrong number"
and transfer-dispute complaints. It reads a complaint in Bangla, Banglish or English, rebuilds the
money trail, classifies the case, estimates how much money is still recoverable, ranks the queue by
recoverable taka at risk, and drafts the next step for a human agent to approve.
